# Code of conduct

This is a personal project for one home that I have put on GitHub in case it
is useful to someone else. Issues, discussions and pull requests are welcome,
and a few plain rules apply to everyone who takes part, me included.

- **Be respectful.** Argue about code, texts and design, not about people.
  No insults, no talking down.
- **Stay on topic.** This repository is about the Hue drivers: what they do,
  what they should do, how to build and test them. Dealer disputes, hardware
  shopping and unrelated software belong elsewhere.
- **No harassment of any kind.** No personal attacks, slurs, sexualised
  remarks, doxxing or pestering, here or anywhere else about what happened
  here. One warning at most.
- **Respect privacy and time.** Do not post anyone's addresses, bridge ids,
  keys or screenshots of their home, and do not demand support or deadlines
  from a volunteer.
- **Say what you mean, assume good faith.** A short, direct "this is wrong
  because..." is the house style in reviews and reports; it is about getting
  the drivers right, not about the person.

I may edit, hide or close anything that breaks these rules and block people
who keep doing it. If something here bothers you, say so in the issue or
discussion, or contact me privately through GitHub ([@corytron](https://github.com/corytron)).
I will read it and answer in good faith.
