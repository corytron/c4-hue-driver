Hue Light device

A Hue Light is one Philips Hue light (a bulb, strip or lamp as the Hue app lists it) shown as an ordinary Control4 light: on, off, dimming, and colour or colour temperature when the light supports them. It is created by the Hue Bridge device's Add Light or Import All New Lights action, normally never added by hand, and it is driven entirely by the bridge: the bridge tells it what its light can do and what state it is in, and passes your commands on to the light. Changes made in the Hue app or with Hue switches show on this device within a second or two, because the bridge listens to the Hue bridge's event feed.

The same device can stand for a whole Hue zone or room when the bridge's Add Zone Light action created it: a zone light, named after the zone (Bedside Lights), or a room light, named after the room with Lights added (Master Bedroom Lights). Its one tile switches, dims and colours every lamp of the zone or room at once, with a single request to the bridge, and shows what they are doing. Everything on this page applies to it; the differences are noted where they occur.

The full manual, with pictures, is on the Hue Bridge device: select the bridge in System Design and open its Documentation tab. It covers installing, pairing, room mapping, importing, renames, replacements, deletions, restores, scenes, programming and troubleshooting.

In Control4

To Composer this is an ordinary dimmer: programming, keypad buttons, Navigators and Advanced Lighting Scenes use it like any other light. A tap on its tile and "Turn On" in programming always mean 100 %, not the level the light had before or the power-on level set in the Hue app. Its on, off and level-changed events fire for changes made in the Hue app, by a Hue dimmer switch or by a Hue automation too. A whole-room command reaches Hue lights at ten a second, so a room with many of them finishes over a few seconds. While the Hue bridge is unreachable, a tap shows the level you asked for after about a second although the light did nothing; the command is delivered when the bridge is back, even much later, and the tile is corrected then.

On a keypad, or on a Control4 Switch V2 or Essential Switch set to Use as 2 Button Keypad, it behaves like a Control4 dimmer once the buttons are bound to its Top Button Link and Bottom Button Link in Connections (listed under its Control Outputs; a single button goes to Toggle Button Link). A tap on the top button turns it on to 100 % over 250 ms, a tap on the bottom button turns it off, and a toggle button switches it the other way. Holding a button for longer than about half a second makes Hue brighten (top) or dim (bottom) the light, the full range in 5 seconds, until the button is let go, as Hue's own dimmer switch does. Held while the light is off, the top button turns it on at 1 % and brightens from there, and the bottom button does nothing. A tap that comes within a second after letting go of a hold on the same button is ignored, because some keypads send one. The keypad's LEDs follow the light's state, including changes made in the Hue app: a top button is lit blue while the light is on, a bottom button while it is off. The 100 %, the 250 ms, the 5 seconds and the blue LEDs are fixed for now: this device hides the proxy's settings, so Composer does not show them. The bridge's manual, chapter 11, has the setup steps.

A zone light or room light is on while any lamp of its zone or room is lit, and its level is the average brightness of the lit lamps. A tap, the top button and "Turn On" set every lamp to 100 %; the slider, or a level from programming or a lighting scene, sets every lamp to that level, so the differences between the lamps are levelled out; a keypad hold is Hue's own dimming of the whole zone, which keeps those differences, as Hue's dimmer switch does. Colour and colour temperature go to every lamp that can follow, and a lamp that cannot ignores them. The tile has a colour wheel if any lamp has colour and a temperature control if any has colour temperature, and it shows the colour of the first lit lamp that has one, because Hue reports no colour for a zone as a whole. Hue takes about one command a second for a zone or room (ten for single lights), so a fast slider drag is sent as one request a second while the tile moves smoothly, and the lamps end where the slider ends; a hold's start and stop go out at once. Turning a zone light off also ends any Hue scene playing in that zone, so a Hue Scene device of the same zone goes off with it (the bridge's manual, 9.4).

Properties

All of them are written by the bridge; the only one you change is Debug Mode, and only when asked.

PropertyWhat it shows
Driver VersionThe build number of the installed driver, for example 116. It matches the bridge's after an update; after an update during which Composer asked Restart Director?, only once the controller has been rebooted from System Manager (the bridge's manual, chapter 3).
Debug ModeOff (default) or On. On writes many more lines to this device's Lua output (the lower part of its Lua tab); developer use only. Remember that a changed property reaches the driver only when you click the row's Set button.
StatusIn normal use the Hue name and the light's model, for example Left Bedside Light (Hue color lamp) (Porch (unknown model) until the model is known). A zone light reads Hue zone Bedside Lights (2 lights), a room light Hue room Master Bedroom (9 lights). Other texts are explained below.
Hue Light ID#46 (e0e51794…): the light's number on the bridge (the same number the bridge's lists show after the name) and the start of its long identifier. Blank until the bridge has linked the device; the long identifier alone for a moment when the device remembers its light but has not heard its number yet. For a zone light, zone #2 (b90ea7b4…): the zone's number on the bridge and the start of its identity.
Hue NameThe light's name in the Hue app. The device's name in Composer starts out the same and is then yours to change. For a zone or room light, the zone's or room's name; a room light's Composer name, Master Bedroom Lights, is not counted as a difference.
ModelThe light's model as Hue reports it, for example Hue color lamp or Hue white lamp. For a zone or room light, Hue zone (2 lights) or Hue room (9 lights); the count follows the lamps in it.
OnlineOnline when the bridge can reach the light; Offline when the light has no power, or the device is not bound, gone or forgotten; blank while the bridge has not said either way yet (for ever, harmlessly, for a light that uses Wi-Fi or Matter instead of Zigbee). While Offline the tile is disabled on Navigators. A zone light is Offline while its zone holds no lights.
Hue RoomThe Hue room the light belongs to, for example Master Bedroom; blank for a light in no Hue room. It follows a move in the Hue app within seconds; moving the device to another Control4 room does not change it. For a zone light, the zone's own label, Bedside Lights (zone); for a room light, the room's name.
Last ActionThe result of the last click on this device's Actions tab, or Forgotten by the bridge after the bridge released it. For a forgotten zone light it carries the instruction, Delete this device in Composer, or bind it again and use Link Devices, because that device's Status is shorter.

Actions

ActionWhat it does
Adopt Hue NameRenames this device in Composer to its Hue name. Last Action reads Renamed device 1073 to Downlight 4; while the bridge has not linked the device yet it reads Not renamed: no Hue name known yet (the bridge has not configured this light). Names are never changed without this click (or the bridge's Adopt Hue Names); the driver never renames anything in Hue. A room light is renamed to the room's name with Lights added.
Forget This LightAsks the bridge to release this device from its Hue light. Last Action reads Asked the bridge to forget this light; its answer sets Status, and a moment later Status reads the forgotten text below. The device stays in the project for you to delete; the Hue light becomes available again under the bridge's Add Light. When no bridge is connected it reads Not sent: not bound to a Hue Bridge. On a zone or room light it works the same; the zone or room is then listed under the bridge's Add Zone Light again.

When this device cannot act (not bound, waiting, gone or forgotten, or a zone light whose zone has no lights) it drops the commands it receives, and Status says why. Colour and colour temperature controls appear on Navigators only when the light has them; brightness is shown in whole percents, and a colour set while the light is off is applied at the next turn-on.

In the System Design tree this device shows a small Hue dimmer switch as its icon if it was created by a build that has the Hue icons; one created by an earlier build keeps Control4's standard bulb. In a room's List View it always shows the standard light icon.
