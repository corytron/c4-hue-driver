Hue Light device

A Hue Light is one Philips Hue light (a bulb, strip or lamp as the Hue app lists it) shown as an ordinary Control4 light: on, off, dimming, and colour or colour temperature when the light supports them. It is created by the Hue Bridge device's Add Light or Import All New Lights action, normally never added by hand, and it is driven entirely by the bridge: the bridge tells it what its light can do and what state it is in, and passes your commands on to the light. Changes made in the Hue app or with Hue switches show on this device within a second or two, because the bridge listens to the Hue bridge's event feed.

The full manual, with pictures, is on the Hue Bridge device: select the bridge in System Design and open its Documentation tab. It covers installing, pairing, room mapping, importing, renames, replacements, deletions, restores, scenes, programming and troubleshooting.

In Control4

To Composer this is an ordinary dimmer: programming, keypad buttons, Navigators and Advanced Lighting Scenes use it like any other light. A tap on its tile and "Turn On" in programming always mean 100 %, not the level the light had before or the power-on level set in the Hue app. Its on, off and level-changed events fire for changes made in the Hue app, by a Hue dimmer switch or by a Hue automation too. A whole-room command reaches Hue lights at ten a second, so a room with many of them finishes over a few seconds. While the Hue bridge is unreachable, a tap shows the level you asked for after about a second although the light did nothing; the command is delivered when the bridge is back, even much later, and the tile is corrected then.

On a keypad, or on a Control4 Switch V2 or Essential Switch set to Use as 2 Button Keypad, it behaves like a Control4 dimmer once the buttons are connected to its Top Button Link and Bottom Button Link inputs in Connections (a single button goes to Toggle Button Link): a tap on the top button turns it on to the On preset (default 100 %) over the click rate (default 250 ms), a tap on the bottom button turns it off, a toggle button switches it the other way, and holding a button for longer than about half a second ramps the light up or down over the hold rate (default 5 seconds for the full range), stopping where it is when the button is let go. The preset and the rates are remembered per light and change through the proxy settings where Composer offers them; the preset concerns the keypad only, "Turn On" in programming stays 100 %. The keypad's LEDs can follow the light's state, including changes made in the Hue app. The bridge's manual, chapter 11, has the setup steps.

Properties

All of them are written by the bridge; the only one you change is Debug Mode, and only when asked.

PropertyWhat it shows
Driver VersionThe build number of the installed driver, for example 116. It matches the bridge's after an update.
Debug ModeOff (default) or On. On writes many more lines to this device's Lua output (the lower part of its Lua tab); developer use only. Remember that a changed property reaches the driver only when you click the row's Set button.
StatusIn normal use the Hue name and the light's model, for example Left Bedside Light (Hue color lamp) (Porch (unknown model) until the model is known). Other texts are explained below.
Hue Light ID#46 (e0e51794…): the light's number on the bridge (the same number the bridge's lists show after the name) and the start of its long identifier. Blank until the bridge has linked the device; the long identifier alone for a moment when the device remembers its light but has not heard its number yet.
Hue NameThe light's name in the Hue app. The device's name in Composer starts out the same and is then yours to change.
ModelThe light's model as Hue reports it, for example Hue color lamp or Hue white lamp.
OnlineOnline when the bridge can reach the light; Offline when the light has no power, or the device is not bound, gone or forgotten; blank while the bridge has not said either way yet (for ever, harmlessly, for a light that uses Wi-Fi or Matter instead of Zigbee). While Offline the tile is disabled on Navigators.
Hue RoomThe Hue room the light belongs to, for example Master Bedroom; blank for a light in no Hue room. It follows a move in the Hue app within seconds; moving the device to another Control4 room does not change it.
Last ActionThe result of the last click on this device's Actions tab, or Forgotten by the bridge after the bridge released it.

Actions

ActionWhat it does
Adopt Hue NameRenames this device in Composer to its Hue name. Last Action reads Renamed device 1073 to Downlight 4; while the bridge has not linked the device yet it reads Not renamed: no Hue name known yet (the bridge has not configured this light). Names are never changed without this click (or the bridge's Adopt Hue Names); the driver never renames anything in Hue.
Forget This LightAsks the bridge to release this device from its Hue light. Last Action reads Asked the bridge to forget this light; its answer sets Status, and a moment later Status reads the forgotten text below. The device stays in the project for you to delete; the Hue light becomes available again under the bridge's Add Light. When no bridge is connected it reads Not sent: not bound to a Hue Bridge.

When this device cannot act (not bound, waiting, gone or forgotten) it drops the commands it receives, and Status says why. Colour and colour temperature controls appear on Navigators only when the light has them; brightness is shown in whole percents, and a colour set while the light is off is applied at the next turn-on.
