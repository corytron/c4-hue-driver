Hue Scene device

A Hue Scene is one Philips Hue scene (a saved look for a Hue room or zone) shown as a Control4 switch: an on/off light without a slider. Turning it ON plays the scene on the Hue bridge (recalls it, in Hue's word), so every light in that Hue room or zone takes the scene's look; turning it OFF turns that whole Hue room or zone off, with one request to the bridge. It is created by the Hue Bridge device's Add Scene or Add All Scenes Of Room action, never added by hand. To Control4 it is a light, so programming, keypad buttons and Advanced Lighting Scenes can turn it on and off like any other; in an Advanced Lighting Scene it is a member that is either 0 or 100.

The full manual, with pictures, is on the Hue Bridge device: select the bridge in System Design and open its Documentation tab. Chapter 9 there is about scenes, chapter 11 about programming.

Why the tile drops to OFF

The switch mirrors the bridge's own idea of whether the scene is active. After ON, the bridge confirms the scene within about a second and the tile stays ON. As soon as anything changes a member light by hand (its own tile, the Hue app, a Hue dimmer switch) or another scene is played in that room, Hue marks this scene inactive and the tile drops to OFF. Playing the scene from the Hue app or a dimmer turns the tile ON. An ON tile therefore means "this scene is what the room is showing right now", not "someone pressed this switch"; programming that fires when this switch turns off fires whenever anyone touches any member light.

Good to know

  • A ramp time given by programming or by an Advanced Lighting Scene becomes the fade Hue uses to play the scene; a tap on a Navigator uses a short fade.
  • OFF turns off every light of the Hue room or zone, imported into Control4 or not; a zone can span several Control4 rooms. A zone with no lights in it is reported off without any request.
  • ON and OFF are one request each and are not retried: if the Hue bridge does not answer within two seconds the tile shows what you asked for, and the bridge's next full read corrects it.
  • Editing the scene's colours or brightness in the Hue app needs nothing here; the next ON plays the new look.

The next ON uses it.
OnlineOnline while the device is connected to the bridge and its scene exists; Offline while it is not bound, waiting, gone or forgotten (the tile is then disabled on Navigators); blank for a moment while the bridge has not answered yet.
Last ActionThe result of the last click on this device's Actions tab, or Forgotten by the bridge after the bridge released it.

Actions

ActionWhat it does
Adopt Hue NameRenames this device in Composer to the name the bridge would give it today: the scene's name for a room scene (Relax), the zone's name in front for a zone scene (Bedside Lights Nightlight). Last Action reads Renamed device 1141 to Bedside Lights Read, or Not renamed: no Hue name known yet (the bridge has not configured this scene) while unlinked. Useful after a Re-link Scene, or after Link Devices matched a device by the scene's plain name, when the device still carries its old name.
Forget This SceneAsks the bridge to release this device from its scene. Last Action reads Asked the bridge to forget this scene; its answer sets Status; the device stays in the project for you to delete, and the scene becomes available again under the bridge's Add Scene. When no bridge is connected it reads Not sent: not bound to a Hue Bridge.
